Team

This month marks an important milestone in our journey. As InfyStrat continues to grow in capability, reputation, and global relevance, we are ready to take the next bold step in expanding our footprint in India.

It gives me great pride to announce the launch of our new company: StratNex. StratNex is being established with a clear and ambitious purpose — to build a strong presence in India’s public sector and select high-impact private-sector markets, where the demand for reliable, secure, and future-ready technology solutions is rapidly rising.

Over the past years, InfyStrat has built an exceptional foundation — global delivery capability, trusted client relationships, and a reputation for quality and integrity. StratNex will build on this foundation, with a sharper focus on local engagement, government alignment, and sector-specific innovation.

Our areas of expertise — Cloud, Infrastructure, ERP, CRM, AI, Cybersecurity, and Digital Services — have never been more relevant to the needs of government departments, public institutions, and regulated private industries.

Why StratNex?
Because India’s public sector is going through a once-in-a-generation transformation. Government departments, public enterprises, and mission-driven agencies are modernizing rapidly, looking for partners who can bring technology leadership, domain understanding, and long-term commitment.

At the same time, we see enormous opportunity in private-sector segments such as BFSI, healthcare, manufacturing, and critical infrastructure. StratNex will position us to serve these industries with a dedicated focus.

This new venture is a testament to your hard work, discipline, and belief in what we can achieve together. Our team has always been our strength.

What this means for us internally:
• Greater opportunities for growth and leadership
• New teams, new partnerships, and new markets
• A chance to contribute directly to nation-building sectors
• Enhanced collaboration between InfyStrat and StratNex
• A stronger platform to showcase our capabilities in India

StratNex will operate with its own identity, strategy, and leadership direction, but it will remain deeply connected to the InfyStrat family. Together, we will strengthen our presence, diversify our portfolio, and create more value for our clients and our people.

This launch is just the beginning. The vision is big, the direction is clear, and the opportunity is real. With your dedication and excellence, StratNex will soon become a recognized and trusted player in the Indian public sector and beyond.
